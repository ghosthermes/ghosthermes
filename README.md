I build agentic systems for authorized security research. My work focuses on the intersection of multi-agent orchestration and deterministic validation—using AI swarms to map complex attack surfaces, and adversarial gates to eliminate false positives. 

### Black Lotus Reforged (BLR)
BLR is an independent research initiative. We engineer pipelines that bridge the gap between probabilistic AI discovery and hard, reproducible evidence. The methodology is simple: deploy fleets of specialized agents to explore, correlate, and hypothesize; then force every finding through a deterministic oracle gate before it sees the light of day.

### Research Philosophy
*   **Discovery is probabilistic.** 
*   **Validation is deterministic.**
*   **Evidence over volume.**
